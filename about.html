About Us

Practical products. Comfortable spaces.

Mswenko Home & Lifestyle is a hypothetical retail organisation created for this academic web-development project.

Our Story

Mswenko was created as a community-focused home and lifestyle retailer. The store provides useful, affordable and attractive household products.

The organisation has branches in Pretoria and Johannesburg. Customers can use the website to view products and find information before visiting a store.

Mission and Vision

Mission

To provide practical and stylish household products at affordable prices while giving customers friendly service.

Vision

To become a trusted local lifestyle retailer with a strong online presence.

Who We Serve

  • Young adults setting up or improving their homes.
  • Families looking for practical household products.
  • Customers who research products online before visiting a store.
  • Local customers who need store and contact information.
