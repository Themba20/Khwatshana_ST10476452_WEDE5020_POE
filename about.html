About us

Practical products. Comfortable spaces.

Mswenko Home & Lifestyle is a hypothetical retail organisation created for this academic web-development project.

Our story

Mswenko was established as a community-focused home and lifestyle retailer concept serving customers who want useful, affordable and attractive household products.

The organisation operates from two branches in Pretoria and Johannesburg and uses its website to help customers research products before visiting a store.

Mission and vision

Mission

To provide accessible, practical and stylish household products while offering friendly service and convenient product information.

Vision

To become a trusted local lifestyle retailer with a strong digital presence connecting customers with its physical stores.

Who we serve

  • Young adults setting up or improving their homes.
  • Families looking for practical household products.
  • Working customers who research products online before visiting a store.
  • Local customers who need branch and contact information.
